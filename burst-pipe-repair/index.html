Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, Winston-Salem Restoration Co responds to burst pipe emergencies across Winston-Salem and the nearby communities. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • In a cold snap, exposed lines that look frosted or frozen over

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing
  • Have a licensed plumber inspect your plumbing system every few years for wear and corrosion
  • Unheated spaces like garages, attics and crawl spaces need pipe insulation on exposed lines

What a Professional Brings to Burst Pipe Repair

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.
