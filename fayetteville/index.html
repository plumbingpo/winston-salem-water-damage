Typical Water Damage Problems in Fayetteville

Not every water problem announces itself with a flooded floor. Below are the situations our crews are called to most often around Fayetteville, and what each one tends to involve.

Hidden leaks: A pinhole in a supply line or a loose drain fitting can drip inside a wall for months. Watch for a water bill that climbs for no reason, soft spots on the floor, and paint that blisters. Thermal imaging and moisture readings let us map how far it spread before anything gets cut open. Even a slow leak matters, because wood and drywall that stay damp will rot and grow mold.

Sewage: When a main drain line clogs, wastewater comes back up through floor drains, tubs and toilets on the lowest level. It is typically caused by roots in the line, built-up grease or a municipal sewer overwhelmed during a downpour. This is Category 3 water, which carries bacteria and should not be handled without protective gear. Porous items it touches, such as carpet and pad, generally have to be removed.

Storms: During a heavy storm, runoff may collect along the foundation faster than drains can carry it off. From there it comes in under doors, through window wells and across garage floors. Floodwater from outside picks up soil, fertilizer and whatever else it crossed, so we treat it as contaminated. A big enough storm can cause it at any time of year, and Fayetteville properties are no exception.

Crawlspace: The space beneath a raised floor is easy to forget, which lets dampness go unchecked for years. Standing water, a torn vapor barrier or a leaking pipe overhead keeps the air below your floors humid. Over time that leads to sagging insulation, musty odors indoors and soft or cupped flooring. Our team removes the water, dries the framing and can point out what is letting moisture in.

Condensate: An air conditioner pulls gallons of water out of the air on a humid day. That water should leave through a small drain line, which clogs easily with algae and dust. A blockage makes the drip pan spill over, wetting the ceiling or floor near the indoor unit. In Fayetteville we often find this only after a stain or sagging drywall gives it away, so a yearly drain check helps.

How Drying Works on a Fayetteville Water Damage Job

Touch is a poor test for whether a wall or floor has dried. The face of drywall often seems normal when the cavity side is damp, and carpet fibers dry long before the padding underneath. For that reason we rely on meters, not on feel. Our Fayetteville technicians carry probe meters for wood and wallboard, scanning meters that read through finishes, and infrared cameras that pick up damp areas as cooler spots.

Different materials let go of water at different speeds. Drywall and carpet give up moisture fast, but studs, subflooring, hardwood and concrete are slow. The layout of dehumidifiers and air movers follows those rates and is changed as each day's numbers come in. Most jobs finish in 3 to 5 days, and if yours will take longer you will hear it from us at the start.

Stopping early is the costly mistake. Materials closed up while still damp can grow mold, swell, or come loose months later, long after the job seemed finished. Every Fayetteville job is therefore dried to a recorded target, and the log stays on file.

The First Hour After Water Damage in Fayetteville

The first hour is when good intentions cause the most trouble. These are the mistakes our crews see most often in Fayetteville, and what to do instead.

Never run an ordinary vacuum cleaner over a wet floor. It is an electric shock risk and it will ruin the machine; use towels and a mop. Do not turn on ceiling fans or lights under a wet ceiling, and stay out of rooms where the ceiling is sagging. Leave fitted carpet in place, because pulling it up the wrong way can stretch or rip a carpet we might have saved. Avoid cranking up the heating, as warmth plus moisture encourages mold.

Do shut off the water, open cabinet doors and closets so air can reach them, and hang up wet fabrics and cushions to drip. Do take pictures before moving anything. Do keep children and pets away from the area. And do make the call early, so Winston-Salem Restoration Co can have a crew on the way to Fayetteville while you do the rest.
