Drying a home is only half the job. Even with normal moisture readings, you are left with opened walls, flooring that is gone and exposed subfloor. Rebuilding all of that is what water damage repair means. In Winston-Salem, we put back the materials that were lost and match them to the originals, turning a work zone into a normal room again.

The scope of repairs varies a lot. On a small job it may be a few feet of drywall, new baseboard and paint. Large jobs can include insulation, whole drywall sheets, subfloor, laminate or hardwood, cabinet boxes, doors and trim. Where we can, replacements are like for like, and if no exact match exists you will hear it plainly and make the choice.

Nothing gets closed up until it is dry. A moisture meter has to show normal levels in the subfloor and framing before new material is installed. Building over damp wood is how mold ends up behind a freshly painted wall. You also receive a written scope and schedule ahead of the work, which spells out what gets rebuilt and when.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • Curing time for paint, floor adhesive and caulk should pass before the room sees water or hard use.
  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.

Our Water Damage Repair Process

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

Verify Dryness First

Our crew meters the remaining drywall, the subfloor and the framing one last time. Until the readings are normal for every material, no reconstruction happens, and no moisture gets trapped.

Signs It Is Time for Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Seams are opening, or edges are curling and swelling, on vinyl plank or laminate.
  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • Ceiling drywall is sagging, cracked or crumbling after a leak from above.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.

Why Water Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

This is not the same as a standard renovation project. The person rebuilding needs to know what the water did, where it went and whether the structure behind the wall is truly dry. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. With that history in hand, problems do not get buried under fresh paint and new drywall.

Matching is the other skill. You will see a visible patch every single day. We blend wall texture, feather paint to natural break points and source flooring that lines up with the existing boards where it can be found. Should a match prove impossible, we present choices like replacing the floor up to a doorway. We also handle permits where the work requires them and keep your insurer updated.
