A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Winston-Salem Restoration Co handles emergency flood cleanup across Winston-Salem and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Winston-Salem

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
